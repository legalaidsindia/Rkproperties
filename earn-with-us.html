Frequently Asked Questions

How can I earn with R K Properties?

You can earn by referring property buyers, sellers, or tenants to us. When a deal is closed through your referral, you receive a handsome commission. No investment or experience needed — just your local connections.

What commission can I expect per referral?

Commissions vary by property type and deal value. Typically, referral partners earn 0.5% to 1% of the property transaction value. For a ₹50 lakh property, that's ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 per referral.

Is there any registration fee?

No! Joining our referral partner program is completely free. Simply fill the form above or WhatsApp us. There's zero cost and zero risk involved.

What types of properties can I refer?

You can refer any type of property — residential flats, builder floors, plots, commercial shops, offices, showrooms, factories, warehouses, and godowns across Delhi NCR.

How quickly do I receive my commission?

Once a deal is officially registered and payment is received from the buyer/seller, your commission is processed within 48 hours via bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS). Full transparency at every step.

Can I be a partner if I'm not a licensed broker?

Absolutely! Our referral program is open to everyone — students, homemakers, professionals, retired persons, shopkeepers, or anyone with a social network. No license or experience is required.
